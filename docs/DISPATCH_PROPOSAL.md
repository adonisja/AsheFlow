Currently, our daily route dispatch process requires solving a complex puzzle every morning: manually staffing 5-7 trucks with the strict requirement of 1 Driver, 2 Trainers, and available Walkers. 

Doing this by hand means someone has to meticulously cross-reference scheduled off-days, ensure no one works the exact same truck two days in a row, and evaluate interpersonal constraints to maintain a professional environment. This manual process is incredibly time-consuming, creates a bottleneck during our crucial morning dispatch window, and inevitably leaves room for human error.

To solve this specific operational gap, I have developed a targeted, automated dispatch algorithm. Instead of spending hours planning, this tool instantly computes the daily assignments while mathematically guaranteeing that all of our business rules are respected.

**Core Capabilities:**
*   **Instant Crew Generation:** Automatically staffs the 5-7 trucks with the correct ratio of Drivers, Trainers, and Walkers from our daily available pool.
*   **Strict Constraint Enforcement:** 
    *   *Availability Check:* Automatically filters out staff on scheduled off-days.
    *   *Rotation Rules:* Ensures no employee is assigned to the exact same truck two days in a row.
    *   *Conflict Resolution:* Programmatically prevents assigning specific Drivers and Walkers together (Ban lists).
    *   *Compatibility & Completion Optimization:* Actively prioritizes pairing highly-compatible crew members (Fav lists). This directly reinforces their willingness to show up for routes, collaborate on delivery reattempts, and ultimately minimizes RTS (Return to Station) packages.
*   **Discord Integration Workflow:** The tool is designed to integrate directly with our current Discord setup. It can automatically post the draft assignments to the "Drivers Chat" and manage the morning confirmation window (handling Yes/No responses and automatic reassignment protocols between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM).
